The Zebra Journey

How It Started

Founded in 2014, Zebra Coffee began with a clear mission: provide exceptional grab-and-go specialty beverages at a fair price. What started as a single humble kiosk has developed into an urban network serving thousands of students, commuters, and professionals every single morning.

Our flagship branch on Mangilik El Avenue represents our latest interior vision—combining swift counter service with comfortable second-floor collaborative workspaces open 24/7 for late-night study sessions. We rank among the top 10th percentile of fast-service coffee chains for customer satisfaction in Astana.

Our Core Principles

Quality
Certified Arabica beans, calibrated espresso grinders, and fresh local milk for every single cup.
Speed
Average order preparation time under three minutes, prioritizing busy commuters and students.
Hospitality
Welcoming atmosphere, respectful counter communication, and comfortable seating across both floors.
