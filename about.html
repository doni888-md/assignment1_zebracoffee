The zebra that never sleeps

Zebra Coffee started as one small kiosk with a simple idea: really good grab-and-go coffee at a fair price. Today our flagship on Mangilik El has quick counter service downstairs and a quiet second floor that stays open all night for students from AITU, commuters and everyone in between.

How a cup is born

1

Beans rest

Roasted locally in Astana and rested 7–21 days before they reach the grinder.

2

Grinder check

Calibrated twice per shift: 18 g in, 36 g out, 26–28 seconds.

3

Milk at 60–65°C

Sweet, velvety microfoam — never scalded. The wand is purged after every cup.

4

In your hands

Usually in under 3 minutes from the moment you order.
