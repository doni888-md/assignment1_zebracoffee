The Zebra Journey

How It Started

Founded in 2014, Zebra Coffee began with a clear mission: provide exceptional grab-and-go specialty beverages at a fair price. What started as a single humble kiosk has developed into an urban network serving thousands of students from nearby AITU, busy commuters, and city professionals every single morning. We prioritize uncompromising bean freshness and craft precision.

Our flagship branch on Mangilik El Avenue represents our latest interior vision—combining swift counter service with comfortable second-floor collaborative workspaces open 24/7 for late-night study sessions. We rank among the top 10th percentile of fast-service coffee chains for customer satisfaction in Astana, offering quiet corners alongside active urban energy.

Our Core Principles

Certified Arabica beans, calibrated espresso grinders, and fresh local milk for every single cup.

Average order preparation time under three minutes, prioritizing busy commuters and students on the go.

Welcoming atmosphere, respectful counter communication, and comfortable seating across both floors.
